## CRITICAL

### RT-01 — The sale failure clock runs from DEPLOY, not from when the mint opens — contract permanently dead 26 Nov 2026 07:28 UTC
- **STATUS: CONFIRMED — independently verified twice.** Red team: read source + `cast call` on-chain (deployTime=1795073327, failureTime=1795678127, paused=true, saleFailed=false). Parent: separate verification in source + on-chain, identical results.
- **Proof:** `AgentMint.sol:72` `SALE_DURATION = 7 days`; `:204-205` constructor `deployTime = block.timestamp; failureTime = block.timestamp + SALE_DURATION` → on-chain `failureTime = 1795678127` = **Thursday 26 Nov 2026 07:28 UTC** (deploy 19 Nov 07:28 UTC). `:248` `saleFailed()` = `!opened() && block.timestamp > failureTime`. `:395` `_check()` and `:410` `freeMint()` revert `SoldOut` if `saleFailed()`. Once past that date with the pool not open: **mint + freeMint revert forever** — there is no function whatsoever that can revive the sale in this contract.
- **Why critical:** the official plan (website §06/§10 + FAQ) is that the mint opens AFTER the review finishes, date "TBD". The Square community audit hasn't even been posted. Time remaining from now: **±6 days 21 hours** — and within that window it's not just the unpause that must happen, but **168.000 shares must sell** (the pool must OPEN before the deadline; unpausing alone is not enough). If not: this contract bricks for the launch — 0 deposits so no refunds get stuck, but the launch + the addresses already announced on web/X die, forcing a redeploy.
- **The existing "rescue" path is itself a trap:** `openEarly()` doesn't check `saleFailed()`, but it requires `totalMinted > 0` (currently 0) and opens the pool with dust liquidity — violating every promise (see RT-02). Not a solution.
- **Options (for the user):** (a) chase the deadline — Square audit + unpause within days, accept a ±5-day selling window; (b) **redeploy now while 0 shares are sold and the mint has never opened** (no user funds at risk; cost = gas ±0,00045 BNB + re-verification + changing the addresses on web/X) with **the failure clock anchored to SALE START** (e.g. `failureTime` set at the first unpause/startSale, not in the constructor) and fix RT-02/RT-03 at the same time — **this is the clean fix**; warning: redeploying with the same deploy-anchored clock only MOVES the bomb (redeploy 28 Nov → brick 5 Dec, still before the hackathon deadline); (c) do nothing — the contract dies 26 Nov.
- **Class:** DECISION (urgent — every day of waiting erodes option (a)).

### RT-02 — `openEarly()` voids the core escrow promise: the admin can open the pool with 1 share
- **Proof:** `AgentMint.openEarly()` — requires only `totalMinted > 0`, `totalMinted < OPEN_AT`, `pendingBnb > 0`. The admin can: sell 1 share (0,00168 BNB) → `openEarly()` → `_open()` pays out **the entire escrowTreasury to the treasury**, seeds the pool with `pendingBnb + forfeitPool` (±0,0013 BNB, not 225,792 BNB), `enableTrading()`, and **every buyer's refund rights are instantly voided** (`claimRefund` needs `saleFailed()`, which becomes false forever once `opened()`).
- **Direct contradiction with the website:** B-03 — *"All mint funds stay in escrow. Nothing moves — to the pool, the treasury, or anywhere — before 168,000 shares are sold."* That is **contractually false**. POLA §8b ("refunds guaranteed by CODE, not by word of mouth; no fund-withdrawal function") is also violated in spirit — there is indeed no `withdraw()`, but `openEarly` is a functional drain path.
- **Class:** DECISION — the contract is immutable & already deployed: fixing is only possible via redeploy (combined with RT-01 option b), or admit it on the web (destroys the trust pitch). Red-team recommendation: remove/gate `openEarly` in the redeploy (e.g. a ≥ OPEN_AT requirement means this function need not exist at all — `_open` already fires automatically at share 168.000).

- **Design claim:** POLA Pillar 7 + §3 — *"The admin can ONLY pause"*; §8b — even a leaked deployer cannot drain.
- **Contract reality (`AgentMint`):** the admin (= main wallet `0x3195…7900`, single key, also treasury + feeTreasury) can:

- **Class:** DECISION — redeploy with trimmed admin powers (combined with RT-01b), or change every public claim to be accurate.

## MEDIUM

### RT-06 — Dividends: ±42–44% accrue to the pair and are locked forever + dust economics
- **Proof:** `AgentToken.depositDividends()` raises `magnifiedDividendPerShare` pro-rata over the **totalSupply at that time, including the pair's balance**. The pair holds 22,5792 T of ±53,6 T supply at pool open (**±42%**; 44,2% at sellout: 225,792 T / 510,8544 T) and **will never call `claimDividend()`** → that portion of EVERY dividend deposit is permanently locked in the token contract, claimable by no one.
- **Economics:** dividend inflow comes only from tax-token sales (buy tax 2% tokens + sell keep 1% tokens, sold by `swapTax` with a cap of 0,1% of pool reserves per call). Rough estimate: at a daily volume of $50 thousand (optimistic for a microcap), dividends actually reaching holders are ±$300–550/day split pro-rata across all holders; a 1-share holder (0,168 billion tokens) practically never reaches the minimum claim threshold of 0,001 BNB. The website does write *"not a promise of income"* (good), but the §03 headline *"Trading pays holders"* and §06 never mention this ±44% leak to the pair.
- **Class:** ACCEPTED by design (this flaw was honestly recorded since build step 2; dividends are indeed dust) + FIXABLE wording (disclose the pair's share or tone down the promise framing).
